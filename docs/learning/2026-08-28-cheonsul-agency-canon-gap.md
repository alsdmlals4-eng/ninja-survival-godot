## Incident

The approved Cheonsul player promise says the player sets up ordered elemental reactions that transform the field. The merged runtime instead alternates `WET` and `SHOCK`, chooses a wet target automatically, and leaves the player with movement plus a charged ultimate. The intended meaningful reaction choice was therefore not represented by the actual player action.

## Solution

User-approved DEC-027 keeps automatic combat but requires player movement and enemy grouping to create a readable spatial condition for a high-value chained reaction. Manual reaction casting is rejected for the first Slice. Implementation remains deferred until the condition itself is separately decided and the Phase 1/Phase 2 gate is met.

## Lesson

For an auto-combat school, “the system performs a combo” is not enough to establish player authorship. The player must be able to point to a visible position, timing, formation, or build choice that changed the combo's result.

## No Base promotion

The lesson depends on Ninja Survival's specific Cheonsul identity, its existing `WET → SHOCK` runtime, and its four-school player promise. It is not promoted as a Base rule.
