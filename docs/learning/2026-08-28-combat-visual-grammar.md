## Incident

Current Cheonsul planning references used a violet-heavy reaction presentation while the approved school identity reserves violet/black for Heukyeong. Persistent text-like status information and always-visible enemy HP also risked hiding the top-down survival field rather than supporting reaction choices.

## Solution

- Lock Cheonsul to blue + amber/orange as the primary reaction family.
- Reserve violet/black for Heukyeong.
- Use compact status icons; retain a future focus/help description path for accessibility validation.
- Hide enemy HP by default and reveal it only for the enemy that just took damage.
- Keep the generated core-scene board as planning exploration until explicitly promoted.

## Lesson

In a four-school survival game, school color ownership and combat-information density are product-specific contracts tied to each school’s danger-processing identity. They cannot be generalized into a reusable Base rule without losing the project’s meaning.
